Local shader adaptation: typed storage images, base array layer,
LDR/HDR output variants, block dimensions in push constants, and
profile-specific error texels. Workgroup dimensions remain specialized.
The LUT header additionally includes cstddef and cstdint.
LUT construction does not decode textures; all texture decoding runs on the GPU.
